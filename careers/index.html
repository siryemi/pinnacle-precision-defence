Recruitment integrity

We charge nothing to apply

Pinnacle Precision Engineering & Consulting Limited never charges a fee at any stage of recruitment, and never asks a candidate for payment for training, processing, vetting or placement. We do not use recruitment agents who charge candidates.

If anyone requests money in our name, it is a fraud. Please report it to enquiries@pinnaclepec.com.
