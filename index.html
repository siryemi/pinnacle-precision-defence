About the firm

Service architecture

Four capability pillars, one delivery chain

Secure, maintainable, auditable and readiness-oriented delivery at every stage — from front-end design through construction, procurement and lifecycle support.

Secure by design, from FEED to IFC

Secure facilities, command-and-control spaces, blast-aware structural design, MEP systems and 3D CAD/BIM documentation — with security and maintainability designed into the frame rather than added afterwards.

Defence engineering design

Built for facilities where downtime is not an option

Bases, barracks, training grounds, logistics hubs, perimeter infrastructure and secure storage — specified for durability against the maintenance budget that will actually exist, and sequenced so an operational site keeps functioning.

Military construction

Accountability at every handoff

Vendor qualification, strategic sourcing, inventory dashboards and controlled logistics for non-weaponized equipment — radios, protective equipment, tactical uniforms, vehicles and surveillance tools — with traceability throughout.

Defence supply chain

From sensor data to maintenance decision

IoT condition monitoring across vehicles, generators and facility systems; analytics that turn failure patterns into maintenance decisions; and the hands-on training that makes maintenance teams actually use the workflow.

What we design for

Readiness outcomes, not deliverables

Every scope we propose has to move at least one of these. If it moves none of them, it is the wrong scope and we will say so.

01

Reduce mission risk

Structured design reviews, quality controls and lifecycle thinking reduce infrastructure and delivery risk.

02

Accelerate delivery

Integrated engineering, procurement and construction workflows improve time-to-readiness.

03

Improve asset uptime

IoT sensors, analytics and predictive maintenance support vehicle, generator and facility uptime.

04

Strengthen accountability

Transparent vendor qualification, documentation and handover discipline support full auditability.

Engagement roadmap

Support today, growing into indigenous capability tomorrow

Our goal is to support the Nigerian military with practical, scalable solutions that strengthen readiness today while building long-term national capability. The phasing is deliberate — each phase earns the next.

See the full roadmap

Short term

Immediate support in hardware selection and supply chain stabilisation, requiring minimal or no structural change from the client. Comparison matrices, technical specifications and a vetted procurement pipeline.

Mid term

Joint problem-solving with commanders — structured sessions mapping communication gaps, mobility limitations, surveillance blind spots, equipment fatigue and maintenance challenges into an addressable solution set.

Long term

A Nigerian defence-industry ecosystem built on engineering, design and manufacturing — local assembly and maintenance hubs, university engineering partnerships and structured technology transfer.

Scope

Non-weaponized by design

Our equipment and supply chain work is deliberately scoped to non-weaponized categories. That is a commercial advantage as much as an ethical position: it reduces procurement complexity and shortens the path from requirement to delivered capability.

Integrity & compliance

What we supply

Radios and communications equipment, body armour and protective equipment, tactical uniforms, vehicles, surveillance tools, field gear, and construction materials, plant and services.

What we advise on

Purpose-based capability requirements and technical specifications — including for drones, aircraft and non-weaponized detection systems — to support the client's own procurement decisions.

What we do not do

We do not supply, broker or finance weapons, ammunition or ordnance. We do not provide armed personnel or guarding services. We do not give operational or tactical advice to commanders.

How we work

All work proceeds within the formal procurement, governance, security and confidentiality requirements set by the client.
