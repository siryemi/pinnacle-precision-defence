What we are

An advisory firm, not a supplier.

Pinnacle Precision Defence sells advice, analysis and programme discipline. We do not manufacture, broker or supply weapons, ammunition or platforms, and we hold no commission arrangement with any original equipment manufacturer. That separation is deliberate: it is what allows a client to trust our recommendation on which capability to buy, and from whom.

Capabilities

Eight service lines, one delivery chain

Most capability failures are not procurement failures. They begin with an unclear requirement and end with an aircraft, vessel or vehicle that cannot be sustained. We work the whole chain.

Define the requirement before anyone quotes a price

We translate operational intent into a written, testable requirement — assessed across doctrine, organisation, training, equipment, personnel, infrastructure and information — so that a tender specifies the capability the force needs rather than the product a vendor has in stock.

Capability development

Acquisition that survives audit

Procurement support built around the Public Procurement Act and Bureau of Public Procurement guidance, with whole-life cost modelling, vendor due diligence and an evidence trail designed to withstand scrutiny from the Auditor-General and the National Assembly.

Procurement & acquisition

A platform on the ramp is not a capability

Availability engineering, spares provisioning, maintenance planning and obsolescence management — the unglamorous work that decides how many airframes, hulls or vehicles are actually mission-capable on the day a commander needs them.

Sustainment & MRO

Training designed against the threat, not the syllabus

Curriculum and exercise design for training establishments and formations — including live-fire and field exercise design, simulation strategy, opposing force construction and instructor development, benchmarked against the threat picture the force is actually facing.

Training, doctrine & simulation

Sensors, networks and the decisions between them

Independent architecture advice on ISR and C4I — how sensors, command systems and communications should fit together across services, what standards make them interoperable, and where a proposed system will fail to talk to the one already in service.

ISR & C4I advisory

Capability that stays in the country

Local content strategy, offset structuring and technology transfer advisory — supporting the Defence Industries Corporation of Nigeria, domestic suppliers and foreign OEMs seeking a compliant route into the Nigerian market.

Method

How an engagement runs

Every engagement is structured so the client owns the output, the evidence and the decision. We are explicit about what we do not know.

Scoping and clearance

We agree the question, the classification handling, the personnel to be cleared and the access required — before any fieldwork. Nothing starts on a handshake.

Baseline assessment

Structured interviews, document review and where authorised, site and unit visits. The deliverable is an evidenced picture of the current state, including the parts that are uncomfortable.

Options and trade-offs

Costed, comparable options against the operational requirement — each with its risks, sustainment burden and dependency on foreign support stated plainly.

Decision support

We brief at the level that decides: programme board, service headquarters or ministerial. Recommendations are written to be defensible in front of an auditor.

Implementation and handover

Programme support through delivery, paired with knowledge transfer to the client's own staff. Our objective is to be needed less over time, not more.

Integrity

Advice you can put in front of an auditor

Defence consulting carries obvious risks — of conflicted advice, of facilitation payments, of work that ends up supporting conduct a client would not defend publicly. We manage those risks in writing, not by assurance.

Our compliance framework

No supplier interest

We take no commission, agency fee or success fee from any manufacturer or supplier whose equipment we may assess.

Anti-corruption

Zero-tolerance policy aligned to Nigerian law and to the UK Bribery Act and US FCPA standards our international partners require.

Human rights and end use

Engagements are screened for human rights risk. We decline work where our analysis would foreseeably support unlawful use of force.

Classification discipline

Personnel security vetting, need-to-know handling and client-owned data. Nothing is retained beyond the agreed retention period.

Export control

Technical advisory work is screened against applicable export control and technology transfer restrictions before it begins.

Our commitment

Nigerian capability, held by Nigerians

The measure of this firm is not how long a foreign consultant stays on contract. It is how much capability remains in the hands of the Nigerian officer, engineer and procurement officer after we leave.
